Decision Paralysis

Food labels give information, but not always a personal decision

Information exists. Interpretation is fragmented. The personal decision remains difficult.
The challenge is not only access to food information, but turning it into a clear personal decision.

Shopping Pressure

Why Food Labels Are Hard to Interpret

Food labels provide product information, but consumers still have to interpret what that information means for their own dietary needs.

Consumers must connect dense product information with personal dietary context under real shopping conditions.

Local Context

Why This Matters in Finland

Food labels are often in Finnish and Swedish. For many consumers, translation is only the first step.

The real task is deciding dietary suitability.

From Requirements to Prototype

What LabelWise Is

LabelWise is a mobile AI-supported system that helps users interpret packaged food labels using their own dietary preferences and restrictions.

Personalized result with explanation, confidence, and feedback

Mobile product input

Barcode and label image input for shopping.

User-defined preferences

Allergies, medical, cultural, ethical, lifestyle preferences.

Hybrid analysis

Rules for known cases. GPT-4.1 for ambiguous ones.

Explainable decision support

Suitability, concerns, confidence, uncertainty.

System Boundaries

Decision Support, Not Final Authority

Decision Support

What LabelWise supports

  • Interpret labelsConnects product information with selected preferences.
  • Highlight conflictsShows ingredients, allergens, additives, and uncertainty.
  • Explain resultsProvides reasoning, confidence, and uncertainty messages.
  • Support judgementHelps reduce confusion during shopping.
Boundary
Not Certification

What LabelWise does not replace

  • Medical adviceNo diagnosis, prescription, or clinical guidance.
  • Allergen declarationsNo guarantee of allergen-free safety.
  • Religious certificationNo halal, kosher, Jain, or official compliance certification.
  • Nutrition authorityNot a dietitian or professional nutrition service.
  • User responsibilityThe final decision remains with the user.

LabelWise reduces interpretation friction. The final decision remains with the user.

Hybrid Analysis Engine

Rules First, AI for Ambiguity and Explanation

LabelWise uses a hybrid engine so clear dietary conflicts are handled predictably, while ambiguous ingredients can still be interpreted and explained.

The hybrid engine avoids two weak extremes: rule-only rigidity and AI-only overclaiming.

Rule-Based Layer

Known Conflicts Are Checked First

LabelWise uses predefined restriction dictionaries to detect clear dietary conflicts before deeper interpretation is needed.

Confidence Score

Confidence Is a Support Signal, Not a Probability

Structured teacher output 
{
  "status": "unsafe",
  "concerns": ["ethical conflict"],
  "explanation": "...",
  "confidence": 86,
  "uncertainty": "..."
}

The confidence score is generated inside the same structured teacher output as the result, explanation, detected concerns, and uncertainty notes.

Not a calibrated probability or certified safety score.

Evidence strength

Higher confidence
  • Complete product data
  • Clear detected conflict
  • Consistent explanation
Lower confidence
  • Missing product data
  • Weak OCR text
  • Unclear ingredient origin

Confidence supports interpretation, but it does not prove dietary safety.
