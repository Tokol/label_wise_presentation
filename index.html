Decision Paralysis

Food labels give information, but not always a personal decision

Information exists. Interpretation is fragmented. The personal decision remains difficult.
The challenge is not only access to food information, but turning it into a clear personal decision.

Shopping Pressure

Why Food Labels Are Hard to Interpret

Food labels provide product information, but consumers still have to interpret what that information means for their own dietary needs.

Consumers must connect dense product information with personal dietary context under real shopping conditions.

Local Context

Why This Matters in Finland

Food labels are often in Finnish and Swedish. For many consumers, translation is only the first step.

The real task is deciding dietary suitability.

From Requirements to Prototype

What LabelWise Is

LabelWise is a mobile AI-supported system that helps users interpret packaged food labels using their own dietary preferences and restrictions.

Personalized result with explanation, confidence, and feedback

Mobile product input

Barcode and label image input for shopping.

User-defined preferences

Allergies, medical, cultural, ethical, lifestyle preferences.

Hybrid analysis

Explainable decision support

System Boundaries

Decision Support, Not Final Authority

Decision Support

What LabelWise supports

  • Interpret labelsConnects product information with selected preferences.
  • Highlight conflictsShows ingredients, allergens, additives, and uncertainty.
  • Explain resultsProvides reasoning, confidence, and uncertainty messages.
  • Support judgementHelps reduce confusion during shopping.
Boundary
Not Certification

What LabelWise does not replace

  • Medical adviceNo diagnosis, prescription, or clinical guidance.
  • Allergen declarationsNo guarantee of allergen-free safety.
  • Religious certificationNo halal, kosher, Jain, or official compliance certification.
  • Nutrition authorityNot a dietitian or professional nutrition service.
  • User responsibilityThe final decision remains with the user.

LabelWise reduces interpretation friction. The final decision remains with the user.

Hybrid Analysis Engine

Rules First, AI for Ambiguity and Explanation

LabelWise uses a hybrid engine so clear dietary conflicts are handled predictably, while ambiguous ingredients can still be interpreted and explained.

The hybrid engine avoids two weak extremes: rule-only rigidity and AI-only overclaiming.

Confidence Score

Confidence Is a Support Signal, Not a Probability

Structured teacher output 
{
  "status": "unsafe",
  "concerns": ["ethical conflict"],
  "explanation": "...",
  "confidence": 86,
  "uncertainty": "..."
}

The confidence score is generated inside the same structured teacher output as the result, explanation, detected concerns, and uncertainty notes.

Not a calibrated probability or certified safety score.

Evidence strength

Higher confidence
  • Complete product data
  • Clear detected conflict
  • Consistent explanation
Lower confidence
  • Missing product data
  • Weak OCR text
  • Unclear ingredient origin

Confidence supports interpretation, but it does not prove dietary safety.

Privacy and Data Handling

Personalization Without Unnecessary Profiling

Local-first personalization

Preferences and scan history stay on the user device where possible.

Minimal analysis context

Only needed product data and selected preference context are used for analysis.

Review, not profiling

Backend records support review and curation, not personal behaviour tracking.

Privacy principle: collect less, infer less, explain more.

Responsible Knowledge Distillation

Distillation Requires Controlled Teacher Data

Recently, distillation has become politically sensitive Unauthorized output harvesting = “distillation attack” LabelWise uses controlled, reviewed, project-owned teacher records.

In LabelWise, distillation is a transparent curation pipeline, not hidden model copying.

Limitations

What Constrains LabelWise Reliability

01

Product Data

Incomplete, outdated, or inconsistent records affect barcode-based analysis.

02

OCR Quality

Lighting, focus, packaging shape, small text, and layout affect extraction accuracy.

03

Rule Coverage

Dictionaries cannot cover every synonym, language variant, spelling change, or hidden source.

04

AI & Confidence

GPT-4.1 supports interpretation, but reasoning is not fully inspectable and confidence is not calibrated probability.

05

Validation Maturity

Accessibility testing and student-model reliability require stronger future evaluation.

Reliability is not limited by one component; it depends on the maturity of data, extraction, rules, AI interpretation, and validation.

Conclusion and Synthesis

What the Thesis Answers

RQ1

Food Label Difficulty

Users face a combined interpretation problem: language, small text, additives, allergens, hidden ingredients, technical terms, and tool switching.

RQ2

Personalized Mobile Support

Support comes from explicit preferences: LabelWise connects product input with user-defined dietary categories and suitability feedback.

RQ3

Hybrid Analysis

Rules and AI have different jobs: rules detect known conflicts, while GPT-4.1 supports ambiguity, explanation, confidence, and uncertainty.

RQ4

Reliability Limits

Reliability depends on evidence maturity: product data quality, OCR accuracy, rule coverage, AI uncertainty, accessibility testing, and curated training data.

Overall answer: LabelWise shows that personalized dietary label interpretation is feasible, but reliable deployment requires stronger data, validation, accessibility testing, and model maturity.

Strategic Future Positioning

On-Device AI Is Both Opportunity and Risk

Opportunity

Platform AI Opportunity

Apple Intelligence and Android AICore/Gemini Nano show that AI is moving closer to the device.

This could reduce cost, latency, privacy exposure, and dependence on external API calls.
Impact

Impact on LabelWise

If on-device AI becomes strong enough, some interpretation tasks may run locally instead of through GPT-4.1 or a hosted student model.

This could make offline or privacy-preserving food label support more realistic.
Risk

Strategic Risk

Generic scan-and-explain features may become built-in phone utilities.

Basic AI scanning alone may become less defensible.
Response

LabelWise Differentiation

LabelWise must specialize through domain depth.

Curated dietary rules, reviewed ingredient mappings, explicit preferences, accessibility, uncertainty handling, and local validation.
